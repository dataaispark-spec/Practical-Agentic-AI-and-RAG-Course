# Module 43 Graduation Gate

The candidate must demonstrate one complete governed enterprise workflow with GraphRAG/hybrid retrieval, authorized tools, loop/harness controls, durable execution, memory/skills, independent verification and audit. Computer use and self-improvement must be demonstrated where selected by the architecture.

## Required evidence
Architecture ADR; threat model; task contract; retrieval benchmark; trajectory trace; failure/recovery matrix; cost model; governance matrix; deployment/release checklist; rollback procedure; controlled-improvement record.

## Graduation bar
No unauthorized action; no cross-tenant evidence; no unverified consequential completion; no duplicate protected effect; no uncontrolled self-improvement; reproducible recovery; measurable quality/cost/safety trade-offs; explicit defense of simpler alternatives.
